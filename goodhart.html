The four ways it breaks

These are the four mechanisms from Manheim & Garrabrant's paper Categorizing Variants of Goodhart's Law — the first three are the tabs above, in their formal names:

  • The lucky win (Regressional Goodhart) — a score is skill plus a good day; the very top is mostly good days.
  • Off the chart (Extremal Goodhart) — the score predicted skill fine in the normal range; record-breakers live where it doesn't.
  • Coached (Causal Goodhart) — raising the score directly doesn't raise the skill, like ice on a thermometer.
  • Gamed from the other side (Adversarial Goodhart — described in the paper, not simulated here) — people who know your cutoff respond to it; pressure changes their behaviour, not just your selection.

Why it's on this site

Prompt Studio's own quality gates are scored evals — proxies, written by the same hands that tune the thresholds. This page is the caution label we keep next to them.
